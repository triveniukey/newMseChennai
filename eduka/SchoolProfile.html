School Profile of Best CBSE School in Chennai

The School Profile of Best CBSE School in Chennai highlights Maharishi School of Excellence Senior Secondary (MSEC) as a premier institution dedicated to nurturing academic brilliance, holistic development, and leadership qualities. Established in 2010 at P.T.C. Colony, Chennai MSEC, is spread across a serene 4.54-acre campus, designed according to Maharishi Mahesh Yogi's Sthapatya Veda principles, ensuring perfect vastu and a positive learning environment.

Managed by Maharishi Shiksha Sansthan, which operates over 150 schools across India Maharishi School of Excellence Senior Secondary, Chennai, is part of the globally recognized Maharishi Vidya Mandir Schools network. Serving over 2,000 students, the school is consistently ranked among the Top CBSE Schools in Chennai, providing a benchmark for quality education.

Academic Excellence

The School Profile of Best CBSE School in Chennai emphasizes academic rigor and conceptual clarity. At MSEC, the CBSE curriculum is taught through innovative methods that focus on understanding, practical application, and critical thinking rather than rote learning.

Academic highlights include:

1. Well-ventilated and fully furnished classrooms

2. Modern laboratories for science, mathematics, computers, and social sciences

3. Institution-designed textbooks aligned with NCER

4. Teaching methodologies such as learning-by-doing, concept learning, presentations, dramatization, and demonstration

Students of Maharishi School of Excellence Senior Secondaryconsistently achieve 100% results in CBSE Board Exams with high distinctions. The school has been recognized as No. 1 among Maharishi Vidya Mandir Schools for five consecutive years in the Secondary School category and awarded the best in the Senior Secondary category for its first outgoing batch.

Faculty Excellence

A highly qualified and dedicated faculty forms the foundation of MSEC, one of the Top CBSE Schools in Chennai. Teachers undergo regular training and workshops to remain updated with the latest teaching techniques and trends.

Faculty strengths include:

1. Personalized mentoring and academic guidance

2. Encouragement of curiosity, critical thinking, and creativity

3. Focus on values, discipline, and character building

4. Inspiring students to excel academically and personally

Consciousness-Based Education and Meditation

A unique aspect of Maharishi School of Excellence Senior Secondary is its integration of Transcendental Meditation as part of the Consciousness-Based Education Program. Students and staff practice TM daily, which helps develop focus, emotional balance, and clarity of mind.

Benefits include:

1. Improved concentration and memory

2. Reduced stress and anxiety

3. Enhanced creativity and problem-solving abilities

4. Holistic development of social, emotional, and intellectual skills

This program distinguishes MSEC as one of the Top CBSE Schools in Chennai, nurturing well-rounded individuals.

Campus and Infrastructure — Top CBSE Schools in Chennai

The School Profile of Best CBSE School in Chennai highlights MSEC's world-class campus and facilities:

1.Spacious, ventilated, and furnished classrooms

The campus environment, aligned with Vedic architectural principles, motivates students to explore their potential fully.

Sports and Co-Curricular Activities

At Maharishi School of Excellence Senior Secondary, Chennai, students are encouraged to explore their talents beyond academics. Recognized as one of the Top CBSE Schools in Chennai, the school offers a wide range of sports and co-curricular activities to nurture every student's potential.

Sports offered:

1. Football, basketball, volleyball, badminton, and tennis

2. Karate, silambam, gymnastics, and archery

3.Chess and strategic games

Co-curricular activities:

1. Music (vocal and instrumental)

2. Classical and contemporary dance

3. Drawing, painting, and crafts

4. Debates, essay competitions, dramatization, and public speaking

Participation in State, National, and International competitions helps students develop confidence, teamwork, and leadership skills, reflecting the excellence of one of the Top CBSE Schools in Chennai.
